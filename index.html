Dr. Katherine D. Sanchez

Gentle, exacting, and a sculptor at heart.

Dr. Sanchez founded Tracy Center for Dentistry in 2006. She thinks of a crown or a filling the way an artist thinks of carving: precise, delicate work, done by hand. She uses the materials she would want for herself, and she works to keep every visit free of pain.

Her patients include other dentists, dental laboratory technicians and physicians, and the practice holds the Better Business Bureau’s highest rating.
